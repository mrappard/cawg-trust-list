== Terms and definitions

The key words MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, RECOMMENDED, MAY and OPTIONAL in this document are to be interpreted as described in RFC 2119 and RFC 8174 when, and only when, they appear in all capitals.

Baseline requirements::
The minimum requirements, independent of credential type, that identity assurance must meet to be recognised under this framework (<<_baseline_requirements>>).

Credential issuer::
An entity that issues credentials binding a signing key to a verified identity, such as a certification authority or a vLEI qualified issuer.

Credential-type profile::
The application of the baseline requirements to a particular credential type, such as X.509 certificates (<<_credential_type_profiles>>).

Designation::
CAWG's formal recognition that a trust governance partner's framework meets the baseline requirements and the designation criteria, for a stated scope and period (<<_designation>>).

Designation interval::
A period, recorded in the designation register, during which a designation is in effect. A designation may have several intervals if it is suspended and reinstated.

Designation agreement::
The agreement between CAWG and a trust governance partner setting out the scope, terms and obligations of a designation.

Designation register::
CAWG's published record of current and past designations, with each partner's scope, period and the location of its trust list.

Identity attestation::
A statement by a credential issuer about who a signer is.

Invalidity date::
For a credential revoked for key compromise or mis-issuance, the earliest time from which signatures made with it are not to be trusted. For X.509, this is carried in the Invalidity Date extension (RFC 5280, Section 5.3.2).

Role attestation::
A statement by an industry body about what a signer is, for example a news organisation.

Trust governance partner::
An external organisation that operates a trust list, registry or conformance program and has been designated under this framework.

Trust anchor configuration::
An X.509 CA certificate together with optional `notBefore` and `notAfter` date/times limiting the period for which it is trusted, as in the C2PA technical specification.

CAWG trust configuration::
The set of accepted EKUs, with their accepted certificate policies and trust anchors, that a validator maintains for `cawg.x509.cose` signers, as defined in the CAWG identity assertion specification.
