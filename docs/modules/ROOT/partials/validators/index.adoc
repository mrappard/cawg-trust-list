== Validator requirements

Inconsistent validators could fragment the ecosystem: the same identity assertion could be trusted by one validator and not another. These requirements aim to make validator behaviour consistent wherever designated partners are concerned.

In this section, the _time of signing_ is the time established by a trusted time stamp, or the current time if no trusted time stamp is present.

=== Loading trust sources

A validator that claims to follow this framework:

* MUST, by default, trust the identity attestation partners in the designation register, using the trust lists the register points to;
* SHOULD refresh the designation register and partners' lists at least every *[TBD]*; and
* MAY let users add or remove trust sources, but MUST make clear in its results when the outcome depends on user configuration rather than the designation register.

=== Designation and anchor validity

A validator MUST treat a credential as trusted through a partner only if the time of signing falls inside *both*:

* one of the partner's designation intervals in the designation register; and
* the validity period of the trust anchor configuration the credential chains to (its `notBefore` and `notAfter`, where present).

If a trust anchor configuration has no `notBefore`, the start of the designation interval applies. If it has no `notAfter`, the end of the designation interval (if any) applies.

A time of signing that falls while a designation is suspended, or before it first started, MUST NOT result in trust through that partner.

A validator MUST also apply emergency distrust entries in the designation register: if a credential's chain includes a certificate with a distrust entry, and the time of signing is at or after that entry's distrust-after time, the credential MUST NOT be treated as trusted through that chain. This applies whatever the partner's own trust list says. A validator MUST verify the register's signature before applying distrust entries.

For X.509 credentials, a validator MUST additionally require that the certificate contains `id-kp-documentSigning`, contains one of that partner's certificate policy OIDs, and chains to one of that partner's trust anchor configurations.

=== Revocation and invalidity

A validator:

* MUST treat a credential as untrusted for any identity assertion whose time of signing is at or after the credential's invalidity date, where one is published, even if the assertion was time-stamped before the revocation time;
* MUST NOT rely only on a stapled status response to establish that a credential has not been revoked for key compromise. If the validator is online, it SHOULD obtain current status from the issuer, and MUST evaluate it as described in <<_evaluating_a_current_status_response>>;
* MUST, where status could not be established (no response, an `unknown` response, or the validator is offline with no stapled response), report that revocation status was not established alongside any trust result, rather than presenting the identity as trusted without qualification; and
* MUST treat an X.509 certificate with no Authority Information Access OCSP URL as not conforming to the X.509 profile, and MUST NOT treat it as trusted through a partner.

=== Evaluating a current status response

A status response obtained at validation time answers two separate questions, which a validator MUST check separately:

. *Is the response fresh?* This is checked against the *current time*. For OCSP, the current time MUST be no earlier than `thisUpdate` and, if `nextUpdate` is present, no later than `nextUpdate`. A response that is not fresh MUST be ignored.
. *Was the credential valid at the time of signing?* This is checked against the *time of signing*:
** If the status is `good`, the credential is established as not revoked at the time of signing, provided the time of signing is no later than `thisUpdate`.
** If the status is `revoked` and an invalidity date is present, the credential is established as not revoked at the time of signing only if the time of signing is before the invalidity date.
** If the status is `revoked` with no invalidity date (routine revocation), the credential is established as not revoked at the time of signing only if the time of signing is before the `revocationTime`.
** If the status is `unknown`, revocation status is not established.

A validator MUST NOT require the time of signing to fall within the response's `thisUpdate`–`nextUpdate` interval. That interval describes the freshness of the response, not the period the response covers, and requiring it would reject valid signatures made before the current response was produced.

=== Reporting

A validator:

* MUST report outcomes using the status codes in the identity assertion specification (for example, `cawg.x509.credential.trusted` and `cawg.x509.credential.untrusted`), so that results are comparable across validators;
* MUST NOT present a role attestation as an identity attestation, or the reverse; and
* MUST continue to use the C2PA TSA Trust List for time stamps and MAY support a private credential store, as the identity assertion specification already provides.

NOTE: Some of these requirements go beyond what identity assertion 1.3 specifies. See <<_changes_needed_in_the_identity_assertion_specification>>.
