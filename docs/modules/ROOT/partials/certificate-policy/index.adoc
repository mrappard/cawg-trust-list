== CAWG certificate policy

This section outlines the CAWG certificate policy. A full policy would be written in the structure of RFC 3647.

=== Policy identifier

The CAWG certificate policy is identified by an OID assigned under an arc controlled by DIF or the CAWG. The OID has not yet been assigned; see <<_open_questions>>.

=== Subscriber eligibility and vetting

Before issuing a certificate under the CAWG certificate policy, a participating CA:

* MUST verify the legal identity of the subscriber organisation to at least the level required for organisation-validated certificates under the CA/Browser Forum S/MIME Baseline Requirements;
* MUST obtain confirmation from the list operator (or a membership source it designates) that the organisation is a CAWG member at the time of issuance; and
* if the certificate names an individual, MUST verify that individual's identity and obtain authorisation from the member organisation, comparable to sponsor-validated certificates.

=== Certificate profile

A certificate issued under the CAWG certificate policy:

* MUST be an end-entity certificate: the `cA` boolean in Basic Constraints MUST NOT be asserted, and `keyCertSign` MUST NOT be asserted in Key Usage;
* MUST contain the Extended Key Usage extension with `id-kp-documentSigning` (1.3.6.1.5.5.7.3.36);
* MUST NOT contain `anyExtendedKeyUsage` (2.5.29.37.0);
* MUST contain the CAWG certificate policy OID in the Certificate Policies extension;
* MUST contain the member organisation's legal name in the subject `organizationName` (O) attribute;
* MAY contain an organisational unit, individual name, or CAWG member identifier in the subject (format to be decided);
* MUST use a signature algorithm on the allowed list in Section 13.2, "Digital signatures," of the C2PA technical specification;
* SHOULD contain an Authority Information Access extension with an OCSP responder URL; and
* SHOULD have a validity period no longer than the period agreed by the working group.

The profile otherwise follows the C2PA certificate profile wherever that profile is not specific to claim signing.

=== Key protection

Subscriber private keys SHOULD be generated and held in hardware-backed key storage. Participating CAs MUST revoke a certificate on evidence of key compromise.

=== Revocation

A participating CA:

* MUST operate an OCSP responder for certificates issued under the CAWG certificate policy;
* MUST revoke all certificates issued to a subscriber within an agreed period after the list operator notifies it that the subscriber is no longer a CAWG member; and
* MUST revoke a certificate within an agreed period of discovering that it was issued in violation of this policy.

NOTE: Because validators evaluate revocation at the time of signing (using stapled or retrieved OCSP responses and trusted time stamps), revoking a certificate when membership ends does not invalidate identity assertions that were signed while the subscriber was a member.
