== Designation

Designation is how CAWG recognises an external organisation as a trust governance partner. CAWG decides which frameworks qualify; it does not take on the partner's operational responsibilities.

=== Types of designation

A designation has one of the following scopes:

* *Identity attestation:* the partner's credential issuers attest who a signer is, to the baseline requirements, under a stated credential-type profile.
* *Role attestation:* the partner (typically an industry body) attests what a signer is within a stated industry. A role attestation designation does not by itself make a signer's identity trusted.

A single organisation MAY hold both kinds of designation.

=== Designation criteria

To be designated for identity attestation, an applicant:

* MUST show that its framework meets every baseline requirement, under at least one credential-type profile;
* MUST publish its governing policy (for X.509, a certificate policy following RFC 3647);
* MUST have a defined process for admitting, supervising and removing credential issuers;
* MUST meet the publication requirements of the baseline requirements and the relevant profile;
* MUST have an incident-reporting process, and agree to notify CAWG of incidents affecting trust within *[TBD]*; and
* MUST have the legal and operational capacity to meet its obligations for the full designation period.

To be designated for role attestation, an applicant:

* MUST be a recognised, self-regulating body for the industry in question;
* MUST publish the criteria by which it attests the role; and
* MUST meet the publication requirements of the baseline requirements.

=== Process

. *Application:* the applicant submits its governing policy, audit evidence and a mapping of its framework to each baseline requirement.
. *Review:* a CAWG review group *[TBD: composition and conflict-of-interest rules]* assesses the application and may ask for more information.
. *Decision:* CAWG grants or refuses designation, with reasons.
. *Agreement:* CAWG and the partner sign a designation agreement setting out the scope, period and terms.
. *Publication:* CAWG adds the designation to the designation register, with its start date.

=== Designation terms

A designation agreement MUST state:

* the scope (identity or role attestation, credential-type profiles, and any industry or geography);
* the designation period, which MUST NOT exceed *[TBD]* before renewal;
* the partner's obligations under the baseline requirements;
* incident-reporting and cooperation obligations; and
* the grounds and process for suspension and withdrawal.

=== Suspension and withdrawal

CAWG MAY suspend or withdraw a designation if the partner stops meeting the designation criteria, fails to report or remedy an incident, or asks to withdraw.

Withdrawal MUST be recorded in the designation register with an end date. Partners' trust anchor configurations MUST be given a matching `notAfter` rather than removed, so that identity assertions signed while the designation was in effect remain verifiable.

=== Designation register

CAWG publishes a designation register listing, for each current and past designation:

* the partner's name and scope;
* the start date and, if applicable, end date;
* the location of the partner's published trust list; and
* the applicable credential-type profile.

The register is a record of CAWG's recognition decisions. It is not itself a trust list, and CAWG does not operate the partners' lists.
