== Introduction

_This section is non-normative._

=== Background

Version 1.3 of the link:https://cawg.io/identity/[CAWG identity assertion] specification defines a *CAWG trust configuration* for X.509 credentials. A validator maintains a list of accepted Extended Key Usage (EKU) values and, for each accepted EKU, a list of accepted certificate policy (CP) values and a list of trust anchors.

The specification requires `id-kp-documentSigning` (1.3.6.1.5.5.7.3.36) to be an accepted EKU, but states that no certificate policy or trust anchors are required for it "at this time." In practice, trust currently comes from the specification's _interim trust model_, which accepts:

* certificates with the `id-kp-emailProtection` EKU and an organisation-, sponsor- or individual-validated S/MIME certificate policy from the CA/Browser Forum, chaining to the Mozilla root store with the email trust bit enabled; and
* certificates on, or chaining to, the IPTC Origin Verified News Publishers List.

The interim trust model applies only to identity assertions generated (as established by a trusted time stamp) on or before 31 March 2027.

=== Problem

Publicly trusted S/MIME certificates establish control of a mailbox and, at higher validation levels, the identity of an organisation or individual. They do not establish that the signer is a CAWG member, and trusting the public S/MIME roots means trusting every S/MIME CA for every subscriber.

Once the interim trust model expires, a validator conforming to the identity assertion specification has no populated trust anchors for `cawg.x509.cose` signers unless the CAWG trust configuration is filled in.

=== Proposal

This document proposes the *CAWG Trust List*, modelled on the C2PA Trust List, consisting of:

. a *CAWG certificate policy* identified by a CAWG-assigned OID, which defines how CAs vet CAWG membership and what the certificates contain (<<_cawg_certificate_policy>>);
. a published list of *trust anchor configurations* for the `id-kp-documentSigning` EKU, each with optional `notBefore`/`notAfter` dates (<<_cawg_trust_list>>);
. a *governance process* for admitting and removing CAs and operating the list (<<_governance>>); and
. a *transition plan* from the interim trust model (<<_transition_from_the_interim_trust_model>>).

=== Scope

In scope:

* trust in X.509 certificates used for `cawg.x509.cose` identity assertion signatures.

Out of scope:

* trust in identity claims aggregation and other verifiable-credential-based signature types;
* Time Stamping Authority trust and private credential storage, which the identity assertion specification already delegates to the C2PA technical specification;
* any judgement about the truthfulness of content signed by a trusted member.
