The link:https://cawg.io/identity/[CAWG identity assertion] lets a _named actor_ bind their identity to a C2PA asset.
A validator can only treat that identity as trustworthy if it knows which credential issuers to rely on.

This document proposes the *CAWG Trust Framework*. CAWG sets the top-level framework and the *baseline requirements* that any identity assurance must meet, and decides which external *trust governance partners* qualify for recognition through *designation*.
The designated partners, not CAWG, operate the trust lists, registries, conformance programs and industry-specific validation.

The framework separates *who you are* (identity, attested under the baseline requirements) from *what you are* (roles such as news organisation or musician, attested by self-regulating industry bodies).
It covers X.509 certificates and is designed to accommodate other credential types, such as the GLEIF verifiable Legal Entity Identifier (vLEI).
