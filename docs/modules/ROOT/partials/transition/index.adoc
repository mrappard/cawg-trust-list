== Transition from the interim trust model

_This section is non-normative._

The interim trust model in the CAWG identity assertion specification ends for identity assertions generated after 31 March 2027. This gives a fixed deadline for the CAWG Trust List.

A possible timeline:

. Working group agrees the issuance model, certificate policy OID and list operator.
. First participating CA admitted and the first version of the CAWG Trust List published, with `notBefore` set to the date of publication.
. Validator implementations ship the CAWG Trust List in their default configuration.
. Members obtain certificates under the CAWG certificate policy and switch identity assertion signing to them, well before 31 March 2027.
. After 31 March 2027, newly generated identity assertions are trusted only through the CAWG trust configuration (including the CAWG Trust List) or user-configured trust.

Identity assertions signed under the interim trust model with a trusted time stamp on or before 31 March 2027 remain valid after that date; the transition does not affect them.

The identity assertion specification may need a minor update to reference the CAWG Trust List and the CAWG certificate policy OID once they exist.
