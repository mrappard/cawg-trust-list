== Transition from the interim trust model

_This section is non-normative._

The interim trust model (publicly trusted S/MIME certificates and the IPTC Origin Verified News Publishers List) covers identity assertions generated on or before 31 March 2027. That date is provisional; an extension to 31 August 2027 has been proposed for inclusion in CAWG 2.0. The framework needs to be in place, with at least one designated partner, before whichever date applies.

=== Planned timeline

This timeline follows CAWG's plan as of 5 October 2026 and is subject to change.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| When | What

| 30 October 2026
| Unified CAWG 2.0 draft circulated for review.

| Mid to late November 2026
| New technical working group convenes to review and approve CAWG 2.0.

| Late November to December 2026

| Mid-December 2026
| CAWG operational as an independent Joint Development Foundation project.

| Q1 2027
| First designation agreement(s) with trust governance partners.

| 31 March 2027 (or 31 August 2027 if extended)
| Interim trust model ends for newly generated identity assertions.
|===

=== Moving partners across

Partners already relied on by the interim trust model are natural first candidates:

* The *IPTC* could be designated for role attestation (news publishers) and, if its list meets the baseline requirements, for identity attestation.
* *S/MIME certificate authorities* cannot be designated as a group, because S/MIME governance is not designed for this purpose. Individual CAs, or programs that supervise them, could apply for designation under the X.509 profile.

=== Effect on existing assertions

Identity assertions signed under the interim trust model with a trusted time stamp before the end date remain valid after it. The transition affects only newly generated assertions.

The identity assertion specification will need an update (expected in CAWG 2.0) to reference this framework, the designation register, and any revised end date for the interim model.
