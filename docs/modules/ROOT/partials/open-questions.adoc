== Open questions

_This section is non-normative._

=== Governance

. *Conformance scope.* Where does CAWG-level conformance end and industry-level conformance begin? This needs an owner.
. *Review group.* Who reviews designation applications, how are conflicts of interest handled (for example, a CA participant reviewing a competing CA), and how are decisions appealed?
. *Staffing.* CAWG starts as an unfunded project. Who does the work of reviewing applications, maintaining the designation register and handling incidents?
. *Liability and terms.* What liability does CAWG take on by designating a partner, and what must designation agreements say? This needs Linux Foundation legal review.
. *Designation register.* Is publishing the register consistent with the decision that CAWG does not operate registries, and who hosts it?

=== Technical

. *Common policy OID.* Is each partner's own certificate policy OID enough, or should CAWG define a common OID that partners assert alongside their own? (A common OID would need an OID arc for the new JDF project.)
. *List format.* Should partners use one machine-readable format, ideally shared with the C2PA Trust List, so validators need only one parser?
. *vLEI.* How do vLEI credentials appear in identity assertions, and how do validators learn which vLEI issuers to trust?
. *Roles.* How are role attestations expressed and linked to identity attestations in an identity assertion?
. *Baseline values.* Maximum credential validity, revocation deadline, register refresh interval and designation period (marked *[TBD]* in this document).

=== Ecosystem

. *Validator adoption.* Which validator implementations will commit to loading the designation register by default?
. *C2PA liaison.* Does the liaison cover sharing list formats, tooling, or partners already on the C2PA Trust List?
. *Deadline.* Is the end date for the interim model 31 March 2027 or 31 August 2027, and can a first designation be in place before it?
