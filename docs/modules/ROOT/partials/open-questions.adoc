== Open questions

_This section is non-normative._

. *OID arc.* Should DIF obtain an IANA Private Enterprise Number, or should the CAWG use an arc delegated by another body (for example, the C2PA)?
. *Certificate policy OID or EKU.* Is a certificate policy OID under `id-kp-documentSigning` sufficient, or does the CAWG need its own EKU, as the C2PA has?
. *Issuance model.* A single CAWG-operated CA, or multiple participating CAs?
. *List operator.* DIF, a CAWG committee, or a delegated operator?
. *Membership definition.* Which participants qualify (DIF members participating in CAWG, signatories of the CAWG charter, or a broader set), and can members sponsor non-member signers such as their employees or contractors?
. *Membership feed.* How do participating CAs learn about membership changes, and how quickly must they revoke?
. *List format.* Should the machine-readable format be shared with the C2PA Trust List and the IPTC list, so validators can use one parser?
. *Deadline.* Can the list be operational before 31 March 2027, or does the interim trust model need to be extended?
